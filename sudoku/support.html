Sudoku Support

wild studio · Free, offline, no ads

Sudoku is a classic number puzzle for iPhone and iPad. It works without an internet connection, does not show ads, and does not include in-app purchases.

Requirements

iOS 14.0 or later. No account is required.

How to play

Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, column, and 3×3 box contains the digits 1–9 once. Given digits cannot be changed. Wrong entries are marked when mistake highlighting is on.

Difficulties
Easy, Normal, Hard, Expert, Master, and Extreme. Each has a built-in library of unique-solution puzzles. Use Preference on the home screen to show, hide, or reorder those cards.
Continue
An unfinished game is saved on the matching difficulty card. Recently completed games appear under Recently Played.
Undo, erase, notes, hint
Undo is unlimited. Erase clears a cell you filled. Notes let you pencil in candidates. Hint explains a standard solving step and can fill a cell; each game includes 3 hints.
Number First
Turn this on to pick a digit on the pad, then tap an empty or incorrect cell to place it. Correct cells stay locked.
Sound
Optional sound for fills, tools, and completing a puzzle. Toggle it next to Number First.

Common questions

Does the app need the internet?
No. Puzzles, hints, and saves are on your device.
Is there an account, cloud save, or leaderboard?
No. Progress stays on this device. Deleting the app deletes that data. We cannot restore it.
Why can’t I edit a number?
Clues that came with the puzzle cannot be changed. With Number First on, correctly filled cells also cannot be overwritten; empty cells and mistakes can.
A hint isn’t available.
If the grid has mistakes, fix those first. Each game has 3 hints. After that, keep solving on your own or start a new puzzle.
How do I hide a difficulty?
Tap Preference, then use the switches. At least one difficulty must stay visible.
How do I turn the sound off?
Open a game and turn off Sound below the number pad.
